## Groups left out by decision (2026-10-10)

Medicine ball (8), Band (118), TRX (31), Rings (23) and Bosu (36) are not
imported: the app has no setting for that gear. Rehab / mobility, Cardio,
Vitruvian and Stability ball were already skipped on 2026-10-05.

Bodyweight plyometric and conditioning moves (box jumps, burpees, jump rope,
jump lunges, mountain climbers and the like) are included with the
Bodyweight group.

## Bodyweight / other (2026-10-10)

plyometric and conditioning 20. Most of the remaining 438 MuscleWiki names
