How much weight after a break from the gym?

Up to about 4 weeks off, you can usually pick up where you left off. After that, take off around 10%, or a bit more after a longer break, and build back over a few sessions.

What the research says

A 2013 meta-analysis of 103 studies (Bosquet and colleagues) found maximal strength essentially unchanged for up to about 4 weeks without training, with losses growing after that. So a holiday or a sick week isn't a reason to start over.

How much to take off

No study pins down an exact number for everyone, so this is a cautious rule of thumb rather than a measurement:

Time offFirst session back
Under 4 weeksYour last weight
4–8 weeksAbout 10% lighter
8+ weeksAbout 15% lighter

This is what GainPath suggests when a lift hasn't been logged for 4 weeks or more. It's a suggestion you can apply or dismiss. Your old weight stays unless you tap Apply.

Then build back

From there, rate your last set each session and add weight when you have 3+ reps left. If the lighter weight turns out easy, that rule moves you back up quickly.

Soreness is the bigger risk after a break. Keep the first session or two a little short.
