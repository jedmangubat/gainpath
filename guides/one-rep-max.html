How to estimate your one-rep max

You don't need to test a true max. A hard set of 10 or fewer reps gives a good estimate: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30).

The formula

Estimated 1RM = weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30)

This is the Epley formula. 80 kg × 8 → 80 × 1.267 ≈ 101.5 kg.

It works best for sets of about 10 reps or fewer, taken close to failure. At higher reps it tends to overestimate, so GainPath caps the reps it counts at 12 when estimating strength.

(The formula gives 97 kg, not 100, for a single rep. It's an estimate, and it's most reliable in the middle of the table.)

Changing rep ranges

The same formula moves a weight between rep ranges. If you did 60 kg × 10 and want sets of 5, the matching weight is 60 × (1 + 10/30) ÷ (1 + 5/30) ≈ 68.5 kg. GainPath does this when you change your rep target, so the next session doesn't start with a weight meant for a different rep count.

Reps left over count too

If you stopped with reps in reserve, add them before you calculate. 80 kg × 8 with 2 left is closer to a 10-rep effort. See reps in reserve.
