# GainPath — brag plan

**What it is:** a free workout tracker (PWA) that suggests your next weight and keeps every workout on your phone.
**Who for:** people lifting in a gym who never know whether to add weight this week.
**What sets it apart:** after each exercise you rate the reps you had left; next session it proposes a weight, snapped to your gym's plates, as an Apply/Dismiss chip. No account, no ads, no subscription, and workouts never leave the phone.
**Most impressive claim:** "Last time you had 5+ reps left — try 77.5kg (+2.5)". The app answers the question every lifter asks.
**Visual hook:** "What do I lift today?" answered by a big lime **77.5kg**.
**Real UI shown:** seeded captures of the actual app: suggestion chip → Apply → LOG → rest timer + reps-left rating → Climb e1RM chart + PRs/badges.
**Tone:** `default`: punchy and clean, in the app's dark Kinetic look.
**Share caption:** "GainPath tells you what to lift next time. Free, no account, and your workouts never leave your phone."
